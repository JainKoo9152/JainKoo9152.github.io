Hi! I am an M.S. student in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at **[Yonsei University](https://www.yonsei.ac.kr/sites/en_sc/index.do)**, advised by **[Prof. Won Woo Ro](https://escal.yonsei.ac.kr/professor.html)** in the **[Embedded Systems and Computer Architecture Lab (eSCaL)](https://escal.yonsei.ac.kr/)**.
My research interests lie in building **efficient computing systems for emerging machine learning workloads** through **cross-layer co-design across algorithms, systems, compilers, and computer architecture**. In particular, I am interested in designing hardware and system-level solutions that improve the performance and efficiency of modern ML workloads.
